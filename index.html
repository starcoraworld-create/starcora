Tap an island

Kids stories, games and colouring from a magical twilight world

Starcora is home to Sola, Oro and Rose. Every island in the sky holds something new for children aged 4 to 7: read along and solve puzzles in the storybooks, colour a page on screen or on paper, and watch the stories come to life.

Interactive storybooks

Read-along kids stories with a little game on every page. Gentle puzzles for listening, early reading and problem solving.

Free colouring pages

Paint online with fill, brush and stickers, zoom in for the small details, or print them out for a quiet activity.

Story videos

Sit back and watch the storybooks play from the first page to The End, plus short clips of the best bits.

Free for ages 4 to 7, with a new storybook every Friday. Works on tablets, phones and computers. For parents and teachers.

Meet Sola, Oro and Rose

Three best friends who live in the Cova, a cosy home inside a giant tree in the Moonmist forest on the planet Starcora. Each one has their own magic, called a Beamdrop.

Sola

The sunny leader and inventor. Her sun-power brings light, warmth and a burst of energy.

Oro

The most enthusiastic little robot in the universe. His moon-power can cool, pull and make things still.

Rose

Small and quiet, and always somehow right. Her flower-power makes things grow, mend and bloom.
