Privacy

The short version: we don't collect anything about your child

Starcora is made for children aged 4 to 7 and the grown-ups beside them. There are no accounts, no sign-ups, no adverts and no tracking cookies.

Who we are

Starcora is an independent kids website. For any question about this page or your data, email hello@starcora.world.

What we collect

Nothing that identifies you or your child. You don't need to give us a name, an email or anything else to use the site.

What stays on your device

  • Which storybooks are finished. The bookshelf remembers this so it can show the next book to read. It is saved only in your browser, never sent to us, and disappears if you clear your browser's data.
  • Colouring pictures. Paintings are made in your browser. If you press Save, the picture downloads to your own device. We never see it.

Videos

Our story videos are hosted on YouTube and shown in YouTube's privacy-enhanced mode. Nothing from YouTube loads until you press play. Once a video plays, YouTube's own privacy policy applies.

Hosting

The site is hosted by Cloudflare. Like any website host, Cloudflare briefly processes technical information such as IP addresses to deliver pages and keep the site secure. We may use Cloudflare's visitor counts, which don't use cookies and don't identify anyone. Our fonts are stored on our own site, so no other company is contacted when a page loads.

Your rights

As we don't hold personal data about you, there is usually nothing to access or delete. If you have any concern, email hello@starcora.world and we'll help. You can also contact your local data protection authority.

Changes

If Starcora ever adds something like parent accounts or emails, we will update this page first and ask for a parent's consent before collecting anything.
