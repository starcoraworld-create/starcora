For grown-ups

A calm, gentle place for kids aged 4 to 7

Starcora is a free kids website full of interactive stories, story games and colouring pages for children aged 4 to 7. It is calm, gentle and made to be enjoyed together.

What is Starcora?

Starcora is a magical twilight planet and the home of three best friends: Sola, the sunny leader and inventor, Oro, the most enthusiastic little robot in the universe, and Rose, the smallest of the three and always somehow right. Their adventures are told through read-along storybooks, kids games, printable colouring pages and story videos.

The storybooks

Each storybook is a short interactive adventure. Every page has a hand-painted picture, a little story read aloud by our narrator, and a puzzle or game that moves the story on. They are perfect for early readers and for little ones who love being read to.

Colouring pages and activities

Our free colouring pages (coloring pages, if you're in the US) feature Sola, Oro, Rose and the plants and places of the Moonmist. Children can paint them on screen with fill, brush and sticker tools, zoom in for the small details, or print them out for a quiet activity at home or in class.

Story videos

Prefer to watch? Our story videos show each storybook being played from the first page to The End, plus short clips of the best moments. They are also on our YouTube channel.

Questions from parents and teachers

Is Starcora free?

Yes. Every storybook, story game, colouring page and video on Starcora is free to use.

What age is Starcora for?

Starcora is made for children aged 4 to 7: pre-K, kindergarten and first and second grade in the US, Foundation (Prep) and Years 1 and 2 in Australia, or preschool, Reception and Years 1 and 2 in the UK. Younger children enjoy it with a grown-up beside them.

Do we need to download an app?

No app or download is needed. Starcora works in the web browser on a tablet, iPad, phone, laptop or computer.

What do children practise in the story games?

Listening and early reading, following simple instructions, spotting and matching, cracking picture codes, putting things in order by size, finding a way through a maze and solving small problems. Colouring pages help with fine motor skills and creativity.

How often are new stories added?

A new storybook arrives every Friday, with new colouring pages along the way.

Should the storybooks be read in order?

They follow on from each other, so they are best enjoyed in order, starting with Book 0. Any book can still be opened at any time.

Can I print the colouring pages?

Yes. Every colouring page can be printed for free from the Art page, or painted on screen with the fill, brush and sticker tools.

Is Starcora suitable for classrooms?

Yes. Teachers can use the storybooks and colouring pages for kindergarten, early years and early grades story time, quiet activities and rewards. Nothing needs installing and no pupil data is collected.

How do you look after my child's privacy?

We don't collect any personal information. Reading progress stays on your own device, and videos load from YouTube in privacy-enhanced mode only when you press play.

We're new, and we'd love your feedback

Starcora has only just begun, and we're always keen to hear from you. Tell us anything you've noticed, anything we could do better, or what your little ones enjoyed most. Email hello@starcora.world. You can also read our privacy page.
