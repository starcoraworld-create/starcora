Moonmist cloud cups

A no-bake yoghurt recipe for kids aged 4 to 7

  • Ages 4 to 7
  • 10 minutes, no cooking
  • Makes 2 cups

Sola, Oro and Rose found the bounciest cloud in the universe on Toffeeglow Isle. Now make your own sweet clouds from high above the Moonmist: three soft layers of yoghurt, topped with fruit stars and stardust.

From Book 5: The Toffee CloudRead the story, then make your own clouds.

What you need

  • 300 g thick Greek-style yoghurt (or a thick dairy-free one)
  • 6 raspberries
  • 1 ripe kiwi, peeled
  • 1 small banana
  • 3 to 4 big strawberries
  • About 10 blueberries, cut in half
  • Half a teaspoon of naturally coloured sprinkles
  • 2 clear cups, 2 paper straws and the printed star toppers
  • 3 small bowls, a fork, spoons, a table knife, a chopping board and a little star cutter
  • Crayons, a glue stick and child-safe scissors

Make your cloud cups in 6 steps

  1. 1Step 1: Make your star topper

    Colour a pair of stars and cut them out. A grown-up can help with the scissors. Glue them back to back around the top of a paper straw.

    Print your star toppers

    A4US Letter

  2. 2Step 2: Make three clouds

    Share the yoghurt between three small bowls. Mash the raspberries into one for a pink cloud. Mash the kiwi into another for a mint cloud. Leave the third one white.

  3. 3Step 3: Stack your clouds

    Spoon the clouds into a clear cup: mint at the bottom, white in the middle and pink on top.

  4. 4Step 4: Press out fruit stars

    Slice the banana with a table knife. A grown-up slices the strawberries. Press the star cutter into the slices to make little stars. No star cutter? Press a bottle lid into the banana to make moons, and slice the strawberries from top to tip for hearts.

    Psst! Oro says one strawberry star is for the cook. Just one!

  5. 5Step 5: Add stardust

    Put the fruit stars and blueberry halves on top, then add a pinch of sprinkles for stardust.

  6. 6Step 6: Pop in your topper

    Pop your star topper into the cup and eat it straight away!

Oh bolts! Did it flop?

Even Oro gets in a sticky muddle sometimes. Try these:

  • Layers mixing together? Use thick Greek-style yoghurt and spoon it gently down the side of the cup.
  • Mint cloud too runny? Use a little less kiwi, or stir in an extra spoon of yoghurt.
  • Star stuck in the cutter? Push it out gently with a finger.
  • Banana going brown? Slice it last and eat your cup straight away.
  • Topper flopping over? Use lots of glue and hold the stars together for a few seconds.

Make it Starcora

Make a cup for each friend! A sun topper for Sola, a moon topper for Oro and a flower topper for Rose. Who will you share yours with?

A note for grown-ups

A grown-up slices the strawberries and helps with the scissors. Children use a round-ended table knife. For the youngest, halve the blueberries and cut the fruit small. This recipe contains milk and kiwi, a common allergy: swap in a thick coconut or soya yoghurt if you need to, and check the sprinkles label for gluten and nut traces. Kiwi turns yoghurt bitter if it sits, so eat it straight away.
