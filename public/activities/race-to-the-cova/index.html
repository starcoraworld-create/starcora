Race to the Cova Board Game

A free printable board game for kids aged 4 to 7

  • Ages 4 to 7
  • 2 to 4 players
  • About 10 minutes a game

Who will get home to the Cova first? Race Sola, Oro and Rose from the Moonmist waterfall, flick the paperclip spinner, and watch out for CLONK! and Oh bolts! along the way.

Print your board game

A4US Letter

Four free printable pages: a colour board and a board to colour in (or color in), each with its own spinner and pieces. They print at real size, so choose "Actual size" or "100%" when you print.

What you need

  • The printed board, spinner and pieces (colour, or the colour-in version)
  • Crayons or felt-tip pens, for the colour-in version
  • Child-safe scissors
  • A pencil and a paperclip
  • 2 to 4 players

Make it and play in 6 steps

  1. 1Step 1: Colour your board

    Print the board. Play on the colour board, or colour in the black and white one first.

    Print your board game

    A4US Letter

  2. 2Step 2: Cut out your pieces

    Cut around the outside of the sun, moon, flower and planet pieces, along the dashed lines. A grown-up can help with the scissors.

  3. 3Step 3: Fold them to stand up

    Fold each piece on the dotted line in the middle (don't cut it!) so it stands up like a little tent.

  4. 4Step 4: Make your spinner

    Put a paperclip on the spinner. Hold a pencil upright with its point on the dot in the middle, inside the paperclip. Flick the paperclip to spin it.

  5. 5Step 5: Race along the path

    Everyone starts at the waterfall. Take turns to spin, then move your piece that many spaces. Land on a special space? Do what it says.

  6. 6Step 6: Reach the Cova

    The first one to reach the Cova door wins!

    Psst! Oro says whoever comes last gets to spin first next game!

The special spaces

  • Ting! Move 2.
  • Whoosh! Zoom up the vine.
  • Boing! Bounce ahead 3.
  • CLONK! Back 1.
  • Oh oroooo! Wind up Oro, spin again.
  • Oh bolts! Back 2.

Oh bolts! Did it flop?

Even Oro gets in a muddle sometimes. Try these:

  • Board printed too small? Print it again and choose "Actual size" or "100%".
  • Paperclip won't spin? Hold the pencil very straight and flick the end of the paperclip, not the middle.
  • Paperclip stopped on a line? Spin again.
  • Piece keeps falling over? Fold it firmly, then open the bottom a little wider.
  • Board curling up? Put a book on each corner.

Make it Starcora

Make up your own special space! Draw a sun, a moon or a flower on an empty space and decide what it does. What sound will it make?

A note for grown-ups

A grown-up helps with the scissors, and sits with younger children while they spin, because the spinner uses a pencil point. Counting the spaces on the board is good early maths practice. Print pages 1 and 2 to play in colour, or pages 3 and 4 to colour in first.
