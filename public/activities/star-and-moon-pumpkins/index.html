No-carve star and moon pumpkins

A no-carve Halloween pumpkin craft for kids aged 4 to 7

  • Ages 4 to 7
  • About 45 minutes, plus drying
  • A bit messy

No carving, no knives, just lots of fun! Paint your pumpkin purple like the night sky over Toffeeglow Isle, then cover it in Sola, Oro, Rose, sweets, stars and moons.

From Book 5: The Toffee CloudRead the Halloween story, then make your own pumpkin.

Print your pumpkin cut-outs

Three free printable pages of cut-outs to colour in (or color in), with easy dashed lines to cut along. They print at real size, so choose "Actual size" or "100%" when you print.

What you need

  • A pumpkin, about 20 to 25 cm (8 to 10 inches) across
  • Purple acrylic craft paint and a wide brush
  • The printed cut-out pages
  • Crayons or felt-tip pens
  • Child-safe scissors
  • PVA glue mixed with a splash of water, and a glue brush
  • A cotton bud and a little yellow paint (or glitter, if you like)
  • Newspaper and an apron

Decorate your pumpkin in 6 steps

  1. 1Step 1: Paint your pumpkin

    Wipe your pumpkin clean. Paint it all over in deep purple, like the night sky over Toffeeglow Isle. Then leave it to dry.

  2. 2Step 2: Colour your cut-outs

    While the paint dries, print the cut-out pages. Colour in Sola, Oro, Rose, the sweets, the stars and the moons.

    Print your pumpkin cut-outs

  3. 3Step 3: Cut them out

    Cut along the dashed lines around each piece. If needed, a grown-up can help with the scissors.

  4. 4Step 4: Glue them on

    Brush a little glue onto the pumpkin. Press a piece on, then brush a little more glue over the top. Keep going until your pumpkin is full.

  5. 5Step 5: Add star dots

    Dip a cotton bud in yellow paint and dot tiny stars all over, or dab on dots of glue and sprinkle on glitter for extra sparkle.

    Psst! Oro says every pumpkin needs a secret star. Hide one on the back for someone to find!

  6. 6Step 6: Show it off!

    Leave it to dry. Then put it by the door or the window, ready for Halloween night!

Oh bolts! Did it flop?

Even Oro gets in a sticky muddle sometimes. Try these:

  • Paint won't stick? Wipe the pumpkin dry first and use acrylic craft paint, not washable paint.
  • Orange peeking through? Let it dry, then paint a second coat.
  • Pieces peeling off? Use runny PVA glue, not a glue stick, and brush a little over the top.
  • Paper going wrinkly? Smooth it down gently from the middle out.
  • Fiddly to cut? Cut roughly around the dashed line first, then go around again slowly.

Make it Starcora

Make a pumpkin for each friend! A sun pumpkin for Sola, a moon pumpkin for Oro and a flower pumpkin for Rose. Then line them up in a glowing row, just like the pumpkin lanterns on Toffeeglow Isle.

A note for grown-ups

Acrylic paint doesn't wash out of clothes, so cover the table and use an apron. Keep paint and glue away from little mouths, and help with the scissors. Once it's painted, the pumpkin isn't for eating: pop it in the bin after Halloween, not on the compost.
