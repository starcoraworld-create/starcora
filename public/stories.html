Interactive storybooks for kids

Each Starcora storybook is a short read-along adventure and story game for children aged 4 to 7, starring Sola, Oro and Rose. Every page has a hand-painted picture, a little story read aloud, and a puzzle that moves the story on. The books follow on from each other, so they are best read in order, starting with Book 0. Any book can be opened at any time, and a new storybook arrives every Friday.

Book 0: Welcome to Starcora

Meet the planet, the Moonmist forest and the three best friends who live in the Cova.

Book 1: The Fallen Star

A secret letter in code sends the trio into the Moonmist to bring a fallen star home.

Book 2: The Star Rocket

The trio build a clockwork rocket, powered by the fallen star, and blast off into the sky.
